Principles

How I Lead

Discovery-Led

Every roadmap starts with real conversations — customer interviews, workflow audits, competitive scans — before a single ticket gets written.

Metrics-Driven

OKRs, CSAT, adoption curves. If a change doesn't move a number that matters, it doesn't ship.

Human-Centered Coaching

Mentoring PMs the same way I redesign onboarding: reduce friction, build confidence, one honest conversation at a time.

Why This Work

Confusion turning into clarity — a customer finally getting unstuck, a team finally agreeing on what matters. That's the moment I've always chased.

After a decade inside Morningstar, Avalara, and Payscale, I kept coming back to the same question: how do we make complex things feel simple?

Calm Horizons is where that question becomes the whole job — coaching PMs through career growth and interview prep, while I explore meditation and content creation as part of a bigger goal: helping people feel more grounded and live more meaningful lives.
